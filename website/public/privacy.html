Privacy Policy — AuraCast Weather

Last updated: August 27, 2026

AuraCast Weather does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or other directly identifying information.

Location

Coarse location (Phase 2, FusedLocationProviderClient) is used only to fetch weather and radar for your area. It is not sold and not used for ad targeting beyond standard AdMob SDK behavior (see Advertising below). You may also use manual city search instead of GPS.

Crash & Performance

Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring (Phase 9) collect device model, OS version, stack traces, and an anonymous install ID for diagnostics. No real identity is tied to this data. Custom keys: ai_engine_used, location_source, tts_offline, active_locale.

On-Device AI & Translation

Weather-report generation (Gemma 4 via LiteRT-LM / AICore, Phase 5) and translation (ML Kit, Phase 7) run on-device. Forecast numbers are fed into the local model; they are not sent to a cloud AI service. Translation models download once then work offline.

Advertising & Purchases

AdMob (Phase 10) may collect the Advertising ID per Google's policies. Consent is collected via the User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK where required (GDPR/CCPA). Purchases (AuraCast Plus ad-free subscription) are handled by Google Play Billing Library 9.1; transaction data is handled by Google Play, not stored by the app beyond entitlement state.

Data Safety (Play Console)

This policy mirrors the Data Safety form declared in Play Console (Phase 12). Contact support@auracast.app with questions.
